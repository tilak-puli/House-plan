# Design brief: Kerala Home, Phase 1

Revision B (pre-construction draft), 30 Oct 2026.

## Site
- **Location:** Guntur, Andhra Pradesh. The plot is **agricultural land**; its conversion to residential use still has to be confirmed.
- **Plot:** 27 ft x 75 ft (5 ft longer than first assumed; confirm by survey), road on the **east**. Planned on this plot alone; a 10 ft strip to the north may be added later. Roof eaves are 2 ft so the north eave stays inside the plot line.
- **Setbacks:** rear (west) 5 ft garden with a creeper pergola, front (east) 8 ft yard (house lengthened to 62 ft; confirm the front setback), south 3 ft service walkway, north 2 ft to the plot line. These follow local practice; the AP Building Rules figures are to be checked by the architect.
- **Compound wall:** precast RCC post-and-panel, 5 ft high (2 in panels in 4 in posts), set just inside the plot line so the 2 ft north gap and the 3 ft south walkway stay walkable; the panels lift out, so the north run can move if the north strip is bought.

## Climate and ground (why the construction differs from Kerala)
| Condition | Design response |
|---|---|
| Summers of 40-45 C | Insulated roof deck, ventilated attic, insulated false ceilings in bedrooms and the office, shaded south and west walls, AC provisions |
| 850-1000 mm of rain, short cyclonic bursts | 2'-6" eaves, gutters to the sump, drip grooves, raised plinth, the open wet area sloping out to the walkway |
| Cyclone winds | Steel trusses bolted to an RCC ring beam; every edge tile mechanically fixed |
| Black cotton (expansive) soil likely | Soil test first; deep footings or under-reamed piles; moorum fill; 3 ft apron; no water or big trees by the walls |
| Hard, often high-fluoride groundwater | RO for drinking, softener if needed, matt fittings and light tiles |
| Seismic zone III | RCC frame, lintel band and ring beam |

## Brief
- Kerala-style single-storey home, in the tropical style of Sthaayi Design Lab.
- One master suite and one second bedroom in Phase 1.
- Two people working from home: an open workstation in the master suite (walls can be added later), and a work table with monitor in Bedroom 2, which doubles as a study-guest room with a queen bed.
- Follow Vastu where practical (the owner has a consultant).
- Good natural light and cross-ventilation, with AC provisions for the hottest months.

## Design strategy
- **Zoning:** private rooms at the rear (west), living at the front (east), services along the south as a heat buffer.
- **Light:** bedrooms, the common bath and living take north light; south rooms (office, sitting area, wet area, kitchen) use deep jalis and sunshades.
- **Air:** vents above the office and pooja doors let air move with doors shut (a sealed glazed flap for the office). Bedrooms have no door vents for sound privacy; their big north windows, fans and AC are enough. Bathrooms have none either. South-west summer and monsoon breezes enter through the south jalis and the semi-open wet area and leave through north windows; the louvred ventilator over the dining and the ridge vents let hot air out.
- **Centre:** the dining hall at the plot centre keeps the Brahmasthan open. It opens to the living, the 6 ft hall and a basin nook with a south window, with no glass skylight; a raised ventilator above it (solid roof, louvres and mesh on all sides, closable shutters) lets hot air escape.

## Construction
- RCC frame with AAC block walls: 9 in outer walls, 5 in inner walls, 7 in walls where plumbing is concealed.
- Plinth 2 ft above finished ground; poomukham 6 in lower; bathrooms 12 mm lower with no raised threshold.
- Steel-truss roof at 30 degrees with Mangalore clay tiles over insulated cement-fibre deck.
- Full details: drawing set `02_Drawings/PDF/KH-P1_Drawing_Set_RevB.pdf`, specification and quantities in `03_Schedules`.

## Front of the house
- **One axis:** a 7 ft gate centred on the main door, a 6 ft stone forecourt with the tulsi thara at its centre, the steps, the door, the hall and the lamp niche all in one line.
- **Poomukham:** charupadi (built-in teak seats with sloping backrests) between the pillars on either side of the steps; turned teak pillars; swing on the north side.
- **Gable:** carved or scalloped teak barge boards and a small brass kalasham at the apex; the gable jali vents stay.
- **Kitchen front:** exposed laterite cladding to match the plinth, its window set in a deep projecting teak frame, so it reads as a solid block beside the open verandah.
- **Main door:** carved teak double door with brass studs, a simple stone surround and a nameplate niche.
- **Lighting:** warm uplights at the pillar bases, a lantern beside the door, lights on the gate posts.
- **Yard:** low hedges screening the sump and the two-wheeler bay; a pair of potted palms beside the steps.

## Proportion and restraint (premium without costly items)
- **Windows:** three sizes only (6 ft rooms, 3 ft small spaces, 2 ft baths); every head at 7 ft, in line with the doors; sills at 3 ft (rooms), 3'-6" (over counters), 5 ft (baths).
- **Sunshades:** only on the sunny south, east and west windows; the roof eaves shade the north, so that facade stays clean.
- **Floors:** one continuous red oxide through all public rooms and the pooja, vitrified in bedrooms, anti-skid ceramic in the kitchen, baths and wet area, granite on the verandah.
- **Services hidden:** pipes in chases or behind one service panel, AC outdoor units grouped on the north wall behind a teak jali screen, downpipes lined up with the window rhythm.
- **Switches:** every switchboard at 4 ft, the same distance from door frames, flush plates in one colour.
- **Lighting:** warm 2700-3000K, layered: wall washers on the laterite and textured walls, the lamp niche, under-cabinet strips in the kitchen.
- **Built-ins:** plain handle-less fronts (J-profile or push latches) so storage reads as wall.

## Built-in ideas, borrowed and adapted
Rule: borrow the function, not the style; build it in Kerala materials (teak, lime plaster, laterite, granite, the chosen tiles); prefer the Indian version where one exists; one answer per need.
- **Baths:** recessed, tiled shower niche with a sloped sill (in the thicker outer or 7 in wet walls); mirror cabinet over each basin; towel and clothes hooks at the dry end.
- **Entrance (genkan):** shoe bench with louvred shoe shelves, hooks and a keys niche; a 3 ft granite band inside the main door marks where outdoor shoes stop.
- **Living:** vilakku madam, a carved teak lamp niche with a light, on the wall facing the main door.
- **Bedrooms:** floating teak bedside shelves with a drawer, reading light and charging point (the 5 in walls are too thin for recessed niches); wardrobe insides planned with saree hangers, drawers and a locker drawer.
- **Kitchen:** deep drawers instead of shutters for vessels, pull-out masala drawer by the hob, utensil rail beside it.
- **Wet area:** dish-drying rack over the utility sink, draining into it (vessels are washed here); ceiling clothes airer on pulleys for monsoon drying.
- **Build-in now:** niches framed before plaster (shower niches waterproofed), fixing for the airer on the roof truss, light points in the lamp niche.

## Vastu notes (confirm with consultant)
- Kitchen SE, cook faces east. Main entry on the east face, north of the plot centre, centred under the front gable.
- SW heavy: workstation with full-height bookshelves and the master wardrobe bay; overhead tank SW.
- Toilets: NW (master ensuite), Bedroom 2's attached bath on the north wall north-west of the plot centre (in Bedroom 2's north-west corner), and a common bath on the south side, south of the plot centre beside the wet area (outside the central zone). None in SW or NE.
- Pooja: in the living's south-east corner, back to back with the kitchen, with the shoe cupboard beyond its solid north wall (no toilet on any side). Opens west; idols on its east wall face west, so the family prays facing east from the living. Floor 3 in higher.
- Plot centre (Brahmasthan) falls in the dining, just west of the ventilator; kept open, with no toilet, column or heavy fixture there.
- Master bed head east; Bedroom 2 bed head east.
- Sump and borewell NE; septic NW; DB on the dining face of Bedroom 2's east wall, away from the pooja and the wet area.

## Materials and look
- Plastered AAC walls with a lime-finish or mineral paint; plinth faced with rough local stone.
- Teak (or seasoned sal / pinkado) doors, windows, pillars and jalis.
- Red oxide or Kota / Tandur stone in living areas, matt vitrified in bedrooms, anti-skid tiles in wet rooms, flamed granite outside; local Chimakurthy / Ongole granite counters.
- Mangalore clay-tile roof with front gable (mukhappu) and timber jali vents over the poomukham.

## Services
- Overhead tank on an 18 ft stand in the SW rear garden, with a small pressure pump for showers; fixed ladder for roof access.
- Sealed septic tank under the two-wheeler bay in the front yard, with a lined soak pit beside it (only about 5 ft from the house and 13 ft from the sump, so a treated outflow to a road or panchayat drain is preferred if available). All drains run along the south walkway to the front.
- Rainwater from gutters to the sump through a filter; overflow to the road drain (no recharge pit near the walls on this plot).
- Inverter circuit for lights, fans, the office, the Bedroom 2 desk and the router; conduit for future solar PV.
