# Courtyard House Concept: design spec

**Status:** drawn. The compact plan (a 42 × 42 ft house on a 50 × 130 ft plot) is in the model and the drawing set, 25 Nov 2026.
**Branch:** `feature/courtyard`. The duplex design on `feature/duplex` is unchanged and stays the working design until this concept is chosen.

**Owner approvals so far:**
- 24 Nov 2026: the courtyard concept; the compact house (tight ring, 42 × 42 ft) and its room merges; the compact ground floor; Group B (elderly parent and access).
- 25 Nov 2026: the compact first floor.
- Not approved yet: the review items in Groups A, C and D (section 13), and the two owner decisions in section 13.

All sizes below come from `99_Source/model.py`. Plan coordinates are in feet: x from the west (back) boundary, y from the south boundary. "Rect" sizes run between wall centre lines; "clear" sizes run between wall faces.

## 1. The idea

Imagine we could pick the plot. The house would be built round a small open courtyard (mutram), in a gentle Tamil style, with a thinnai at the front. Every feature and decision from the duplex carries over unless this spec says otherwise.

| Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Storeys | Ground floor round the court, plus one upper wing on the west and north-west | All daily life stays on one level. The bedrooms upstairs get privacy. The courtyard still gets sun. |
| Courtyard | Open to the sky, with a steel grille at the inner eave and a pull-across shade cloth | Rain and night sky inside the house, the most cooling at night, and secure. |
| Plan shape | A ring of rooms round the court | A true mutram on the brahmasthan. Rejected: a U open to the east (the court loses privacy) and a deep agraharam (the court is too small). |
| Size (24 Nov 2026) | Compact: 42 × 42 ft, rooms in 12'-6" bands, a 3'-6" walkway, a 10 × 10 ft court | The 54 × 54 ft house was about 70% bigger than the duplex. The compact house is about the duplex's size. |
| Merges (24 Nov 2026) | Genkan and entry hall become one foyer. The oonjal moves into a nook open to the family room. Living and dining become one L-shaped family room. The upstairs reading room and theatre become one upstairs family room. | These merges let the plan fit in 42 ft. |

## 6. Materials and character

- **Walls:** AAC blocks with lime plaster in warm white and ochre. 9 in on the outside, 7 in where there is plumbing, 5 in inside.
- **Floors:**
  - Athangudi handmade tiles in the family room (living, console nook, oonjal link, dining end).
  - Red oxide in the master, the den, the stair, the pooja and the thinnai.
  - Rough-dressed stone in the courtyard, the walkway and the foyer.
  - Matt vitrified tiles in the kitchen, Bedroom 2's suite and the upstairs family room.
  - Anti-skid ceramic in the baths.
- **Woodwork:** teak for the carved, brass-studded main door, the pillars, the jharokhas, the jali screens and the internal doors.
- **Details:** kolam stones at the main thresholds, oil-lamp niches, brass hardware, the uruli niche, and rain chains into pebble basins.
- **Light:** warm and layered: lamp sockets switched from the door switch, dimmable ceiling lights, and low motion lights on the stair.

## 7. Climate (Guntur)

- **By day:** hot air rises out of the courtyard and draws air through the rooms. The two-storey west wing shades the court from the afternoon sun, and the walkway shades every wall that faces it.
- **At night:** cooler air settles into the courtyard. Windows facing it stay open behind the grille.
- **AC:** provided in five rooms: the master, the den, the family room, Bedroom 2 and the upstairs family room. The doors onto the walkway close when the AC runs.
- **Monsoon:** the covered walkway links every room. The court has two drains sized for a 100 mm-per-hour downpour, plus an independent overflow.

## 8. Services

- **Water:**
  - The sump on the north strip takes the roof and courtyard rain through the first-flush filter. It is topped up from the road main.
  - It feeds two interconnected 500 L tanks on a 3 ft steel stand at the terrace's north-west corner, under the canopy. The stand sits inside the west wall and the north parapet, clear of the corner canopy post and 1 in clear of the slim washer cabinet and the sink. A pressure pump serves the showers.
- **Soil:** to the sealed septic tank south-east of the forecourt, with its effluent going to the soak pit.
- **Grey water:** bath grey water goes to the soak pit. Kitchen grey water goes through a grease trap to the banana circle. The terrace laundry drains down its own duct to the grey line.
- **Stacks:**
  - Bedroom 2's WC goes straight out through the west wall.
  - The upper toilet has its own stack in the den bath's corner chase.
  - The den bath's WC goes out through the north wall.
  - The master and powder-room WCs each run in one unjointed length to the south wall.
- **Crossings:** at every crossing the soil pipe runs lowest and the upper pipe is sleeved.
- **Electrical:**
  - The main DB is in the cupboard under flight 2, with a sub-board on the gallery's east wall.
  - The LiFePO4 inverter stands outside the south wall at the kitchen's south-west corner, in an IP54 heat-shielded box 3.5 ft or more from every opening.
  - Interlinked smoke alarms at the stair foot and head and in the master, the den and Bedroom 2. A heat alarm in the kitchen.
  - Pull-cord alarms in the master and den baths sound in the kitchen and the den.

## 13. Open items not yet approved

These come from the four room reviews of the 54 × 54 ft house, re-checked against the compact plan. Findings that the compact plan or Group B already resolves are left out. Each item below is a proposal for the owner. The full list is also in the workbook's Open_Issues tab.

**Owner decisions:**
1. **Terrace step at D49.** The terrace slab (+11'-11") is only 1 in below the upstairs family room (+12'-0"). That is too little for a waterproofing upturn and a weather sill. A deeper step lowers the slab, and then every terrace edge-wall height has to be re-derived.
   - It already has a cost: the living has no false ceiling. Its ceiling is the slab's plastered soffit at about +11'-0" (9'-0" clear over the +2'-0" floor), so the slab, its 1:80 falls, the membrane and the terracotta tile fit under the +11'-11" terrace. A deeper step costs more headroom. Raising the terrace a riser instead would change the 16-riser stair.
2. **Two deviations to see.**
   - W37 (the escape window) and W31 in Bedroom 2's east wall have moved. W37 is now 2'-2" wide (sill 3'-0"), its sill 9 in above the tiles at its north jamb over a 6 in flashed apron upstand (A-501/10). W31 is narrowed to 1'-6". This keeps both clear of the ring roof.
   - There is no second downpipe on the ring's south side: its gutter drains only to the downpipe at x 49. A second one would cross the soil and grey lines in the south pipe strip. The gutter is the standard 150 mm (not an upgrade), so the engineer is to check the run or upsize it to 200 mm.

**Group A: safety and water**
- **Thinnai and main door in driven rain.** The foyer is at the thinnai level, with only a weather bar at D01. Proposed: a 3 in sill, a scupper, rough-trowelled red oxide near the steps and door, and chiks or louvres between the pillars in October–November.
- **Utility service steps.** The landing was 1'-3" deep, and the risers did not reach the ground. Now drawn as proposed, for the owner to confirm: a 3 ft landing one riser below the utility floor, four risers of about 5.9 in in all on 10.8 in treads down along the wall to the east, and an SS grab rail on the wall. The kitchen line's chamber IC-22 moves east, clear of the steps.
- **Courtyard edge at night.** There is a 5 in unguarded drop and an 18 in kerb, both in rough granite. Proposed: a contrasting nosing and a step-light strip.
- **Pooja.** There is a 3 in step, and lamps burn with no heat alarm or extinguisher near. Proposed: a 1.5 in sill or a jamb rail, a heat alarm and an extinguisher.
- **Kitchen hood outlet.** It goes through W83, about 2'-6" from the LPG cage. Proposed: a cowl that throws the exhaust up and away.
- **Escape window W37.** It opens onto the master bath's tiled roof, with no way down. Proposed: a note and a hold-fast or ladder bracket.
- **Main DB.** Proposed: keep 1 m in front of it clear of linen shelves.
- **Terrace canopy in cyclones.** Proposed: the frame and tile fixings designed for uplift, or a metal-sheet canopy.

**Group C: comfort**
- **Family room AC.** It is one unit for an L of about 430 sq ft, and the foyer's 4 ft opening never closes. Proposed: a sliding leaf in the opening, and the unit sized by the MEP engineer.
- **Sound round the den.**
  - The living's TV console is on the den's east wall, behind the den's sofa-bed.
  - The upstairs family room with its screen is above the den.
  - Proposed: mineral wool and board on the den side of that wall, and a floating floor upstairs.
