# Duplex option: design spec

Branch `feature/duplex`. A trial to see how much plot length a two-level house actually needs. Main keeps the single-storey design for comparison.

Current version, 2 Nov 2026, after these reviews:
- Double height dropped; a covered terrace was added over the front half.
- The outdoor lounge was merged into the upper master.
- The space under the stair was opened to the lobby.
- The ridge ventilator was removed.
- The house was lengthened from 44 to 50 ft.

## Brief

- Same plot and road (east). The house and its yards use the front **61 ft** (x 14–75). The back 14 ft (x 0–14) stays spare land: kitchen garden, septic tank and soak pit.
- 3 bedrooms:
  - **Ground floor:** a master bedroom with an attached bath, and a small bedroom that uses the common bath.
  - **First floor:** a second master suite with its own bath, dressing room, and a sitting and study zone.
- **First floor, front half:** a covered terrace under the main roof, carried on a light steel frame.
- No double height. A slab covers the whole two-storey block, so every ground-floor room has a flat ceiling at about 9'-5".

## Levels and stair

- Ground FFL +2 ft above ground. Floor-to-floor height 10 ft, so the first floor is at +12 ft above ground.
- **Stair:** dog-leg, 3'-6" flights, 17 risers of about 7 in, 10 in treads, 9 + 8 risers with a half landing at the north end. The foot is at the lobby.
- **Terrace:** waterproofed slab falling 1:80 to a drain in the south-east corner. Steel posts stand on RCC stubs 6 in above the tiles.

## Roof

- **Main roof:** one hipped clay-tile roof over the whole two-storey block (x 15–63 at the eaves). It sits on walls over the back rooms and on a light steel frame over the terrace.
- **Front gable:** a lower gable over the single-storey poomukham and kitchen front, facing the road. Together they read as a two-tier Kerala roof.
- **No ridge ventilator:** hot air under the roof escapes at the terrace's open sides. The attic over the upper rooms has meshed ridge and soffit vents.
- **Option:** the terrace part of the roof could use an insulated metal sheet on the same steel frame, to save cost.
- **Eaves:** 2 ft overhang, gutters on both roofs, 6 downpipes to the sump.

## Services

- **Wet rooms:** all three baths sit in the south band. The upper bath stacks over the common bath, and its WC stack runs down the wet wall by the store.
- **Septic tank and soak pit:** in the spare rear land, about 10 ft from the house and far from the sump.
- **Water tank:** a 1,000 L loft tank over the upper bath, filled from the sump, with a pressure pump.
- **AC:** the three bedrooms (the upper suite takes 1.5 ton). The living can take one later.

## What stays from the single-storey design

- The front elevation: gable, pillars, charupadi seats, swing, laterite kitchen front.
- Materials, finishes and the look selector.
- The thin precast compound wall.
