# Room-by-room detailing guide

Kerala Home, Phase 1, Guntur, Andhra Pradesh (agricultural land: conversion to be confirmed). Revision C-duplex (option study), 1 Nov 2026.

This guide lists the mistakes people most often regret in Indian homes, room by room, and what this design does instead.
"Shown on" points to the sheet in the drawing set (A-501/3 = detail 3 on sheet A-501).
Go through it with your architect, engineer and contractor; the same list is in the Excel workbook (Checklist tab) with a status column.

Site conditions that drive many of these points (Guntur): very hot summers (40-45 C), short heavy cyclonic rain,
expansive black cotton soil, hard and often fluoride-rich groundwater, seismic zone III, termites and mosquitoes.

## Whole house: structure and site (Guntur)
1. **Avoid:** Ordinary shallow footings on black cotton soil: walls crack after the first monsoon as the soil swells and shrinks.  
   **Do:** Get a soil test first. Take footings below the active zone or use under-reamed piles, tie them with a plinth beam, and put a sand cushion under it.  
   *Shown on: A-301, A-501/1*
2. **Avoid:** Filling the plinth with the black soil dug out of the foundations: the floor heaves and cracks.  
   **Do:** Fill with moorum or gravel compacted in 150 mm layers. Cart the black soil away or use it in the garden.  
   *Shown on: A-501/1*
3. **Avoid:** Rainwater, garden beds and soak pits right next to the walls.  
   **Do:** Lay a 3 ft paved apron sloping away, run gutters to the sump, and keep soak pits and septic 6 ft+ away (here the sealed septic tank and lined soak pit sit in the spare rear land, about 15 ft from the house).  
   *Shown on: A-101, P-101*
4. **Avoid:** Planting neem, mango or coconut trees close to the house for shade: roots dry the soil and the walls crack.  
   **Do:** Keep big trees 8 ft+ away (ideally their mature height). Shade the west wall with a creeper pergola instead.  
   *Shown on: A-101*
5. **Avoid:** Plinth set at road level on farm land: roads and neighbouring plots get raised later and rain flows in.  
   **Do:** FFL 2 ft above finished ground and at least 18 in above the road crown. Survey levels before fixing it.  
   *Shown on: A-001, A-301*
6. **Avoid:** Skipping the anti-termite treatment or doing it after the floor.  
   **Do:** Treat before the plinth fill, at each fill layer and along the walls; get a certificate with a warranty.  
   *Shown on: A-001*
7. **Avoid:** Treating AAC like brick: no mesh at joints, chases cut with a hammer. Hairline cracks appear everywhere.  
   **Do:** Put a mesh strip over every block-to-concrete joint, cut chases with a machine, and use thin-bed mortar.  
   *Shown on: A-502/8*
8. **Avoid:** No lintel band or ring beam: risky in seismic zone III, and the roof trusses have nothing solid to bolt to.  
   **Do:** Run a continuous RCC lintel band at door height and a ring beam at wall top with holding-down bolts.  
   *Shown on: A-301, A-501/4*
9. **Avoid:** No record of where pipes and wires run: someone drills into a pipe years later.  
   **Do:** Photograph every wall with a tape measure in view before plaster and keep the photos with the drawings.  
   *Shown on: A-001*
10. **Avoid:** Designing the footings and columns for one storey and adding the first floor later.  
   **Do:** This duplex is two storeys from day one: the engineer sizes footings, columns and the first-floor slab for both floors, with RCC column stubs at the terrace edge for the steel roof posts.  
   *Shown on: A-301*

## Poomukham and entrance
11. **Avoid:** Polished granite on steps and verandah: slippery when wet.  
   **Do:** Use flamed or leather-finish granite and give steps a nosing.  
   *Shown on: A-108*
12. **Avoid:** Uneven steps (the last one different), which make people trip.  
   **Do:** Make all risers 6 in and treads 12 in, with 3 equal risers from ground to verandah.  
   *Shown on: A-202, A-301*
13. **Avoid:** Verandah floor at the same level as the house: wind-driven rain runs in under the main door.  
   **Do:** Set the poomukham 6 in lower, sloping out 1:100, with a raised weather bar under the main door.  
   *Shown on: A-108, A-502/9*
14. **Avoid:** No place to wash feet or leave footwear: mud and dust come inside.  
   **Do:** Put a foot-wash tap with a small drain beside the steps, and keep a shoe bench just inside the main door (here on the east wall north of the door).  
   *Shown on: A-104*
15. **Avoid:** Oonjal (swing) hung later from the roof purlins.  
   **Do:** Cast or bolt swing hooks into the verandah beam, rated for 200 kg, before the roof goes on.  
   *Shown on: A-104*
16. **Avoid:** Bell, CCTV and outdoor light added later with surface wiring.  
   **Do:** Run conduits now to the bell, camera, gate light and intercom.  
   *Shown on: E-101, E-103*

## Living
17. **Avoid:** TV on a wall facing a window, so glare washes it out.  
   **Do:** TV on the stair wall (west) of the living, the north window to the side, nothing bright behind or opposite the screen; conduit to the router under the stair.  
   *Shown on: A-104, E-103*
18. **Avoid:** Too few sockets, leading to extension cords across the floor.  
   **Do:** Fit sockets at the sofa end and 2 at the TV, and the router socket on the inverter circuit.  
   *Shown on: E-103*
19. **Avoid:** Living under a bare slab that heats up in the afternoon.  
   **Do:** The slab over the living is the covered terrace, shaded by the main roof, so it stays cool; insulate under the terrace tiles if the terrace is ever left open to the sky.  
   *Shown on: A-301, A-109*
20. **Avoid:** A dark living with only one outside wall to the north.  
   **Do:** Light from the north window, the main door and the poomukham, borrowed light through the stair jali; light wall and ceiling colours.  
   *Shown on: A-102*
21. **Avoid:** Large west or south glazing: the room overheats in April-May.  
   **Do:** Glaze to the north; light from the east comes in through the main door and poomukham; south only through jalis and sunshaded windows.  
   *Shown on: A-201*
22. **Avoid:** Main door swinging into the sofa or the DB.  
   **Do:** Check door swings against the furniture; the DB is in the under-stair cupboard off the lobby, away from the door and the pooja.  
   *Shown on: A-104, E-103*

## Stair and first floor
23. **Avoid:** Uneven risers or a short landing: people trip, furniture cannot go up.  
   **Do:** 17 equal risers of about 7 in, 10 in treads, 3'-6" flights and a half landing about 7'-6" x 5'-8"; check a mattress and a wardrobe panel can turn on it.  
   *Shown on: A-102, A-301*
24. **Avoid:** Low headroom where the upper flight passes over the lobby.  
   **Do:** Keep 7 ft clear under every part of the stair; the space under the upper flight is a cupboard, not a walkway.  
   *Shown on: A-301*
25. **Avoid:** Open stair edges and a lounge rail children can climb.  
   **Do:** Railings 3'-6" high with vertical members at 4 in gaps, no horizontal rails to climb.  
   *Shown on: A-103*
26. **Avoid:** First-floor outdoor lounge leaking into the bedroom below.  
   **Do:** Waterproof the slab with upturns, fall 1:80 to a spout away from the wall, flood-test 48 h, then tile.  
   *Shown on: A-109*
27. **Avoid:** West sun on the upper floor.  
   **Do:** The upper master's west wall is solid; the lounge's open west side gets bamboo chiks; the main roof's 2 ft eave shades the walls.  
   *Shown on: A-202*
28. **Avoid:** Covered terrace leaking into the living below.  
   **Do:** Waterproof the whole terrace slab with upturns at the walls and posts, fall 1:80 to the drain in the south-east corner, flood-test 48 h, then tile.  
   *Shown on: A-109, P-101*
29. **Avoid:** Steel roof posts rusting at the base where water sits.  
   **Do:** Hot-dip galvanised posts on RCC stubs 6 in above the terrace tiles, base plates grouted and sealed; repaint every few years.  
   *Shown on: A-301*
30. **Avoid:** Rain blowing onto the terrace in the monsoon.  
   **Do:** The main roof overhangs 2 ft beyond the railings; add bamboo chiks or roll-down blinds on the south and east if needed.  
   *Shown on: A-202*

## Ventilator on the ridge
31. **Avoid:** Hot air trapped under the tiles over the open terrace.  
   **Do:** A raised ventilator on the ridge with louvres on all four sides lets it out; the terrace below is open-sided, so no shutters are needed.  
   *Shown on: A-503, A-110*
32. **Avoid:** Flat-topped roof ventilator: water ponds, leaves and droppings collect, and it leaks first.  
   **Do:** Give it a small hipped roof at the main roof pitch, tiled and clipped, with a 1'-6" overhang and drip edge.  
   *Shown on: A-503*
33. **Avoid:** Flat or shallow louvres: cyclone rain blows straight through.  
   **Do:** 45 deg blades sloping down and out with a 4 in lap (no straight line through), on a sill sloped out 1:10 with a drip.  
   *Shown on: A-503*
34. **Avoid:** Birds nesting in the ventilator.  
   **Do:** SS 304 bird mesh behind the louvres in a continuous frame.  
   *Shown on: A-503*

## Kitchen
35. **Avoid:** Counter at the Western 36 in height: tiring for Indian cooking with tall vessels.  
   **Do:** Make the counter 34 in, with a 30-31 in section for grinding and rolling chapatis.  
   *Shown on: A-502/6*
36. **Avoid:** White glossy floor and counter: shows every turmeric stain and is slippery with oil.  
   **Do:** Use a mid-tone matt floor and a granite counter (local Chimakurthy / Ongole).  
   *Shown on: A-108*
37. **Avoid:** Chimney duct with long bends: weak suction and grease build-up.  
   **Do:** Hob on the east run with the chimney ducted straight out through the east wall.  
   *Shown on: A-502/6, E-103*
38. **Avoid:** Too few counter sockets.  
   **Do:** Fit 5-6 sockets at 42 in plus 16 A for the fridge, microwave / OTG and chimney; RO point by the sink.  
   *Shown on: E-103*
39. **Avoid:** Base cabinets standing on the floor: rot and termites.  
   **Do:** Put cabinets on 4 in legs or a granite plinth.  
   *Shown on: A-502/6*
40. **Avoid:** Gas cylinder in a closed cabinet under the hob.  
   **Do:** Keep the cylinder in the wet area with a fixed copper / steel line and a valve through the wall.  
   *Shown on: A-104*
41. **Avoid:** Kitchen drain without a grease trap: chokes within a year.  
   **Do:** Put a grease trap outside, before any junction.  
   *Shown on: P-101*
42. **Avoid:** Fridge beside the hob or in direct sun.  
   **Do:** Fridge on the kitchen's north wall, clear of the entry door and away from the hob.  
   *Shown on: A-104*

## Wet area, store and kitchen entry
43. **Avoid:** A narrow kitchen entry squeezed past a TV wall: two people can't pass and serving is awkward.  
   **Do:** The kitchen is entered through a 3 ft hinged door on its west side, straight off the dining; the wet area opens off the kitchen through a sliding door.  
   *Shown on: A-102*
44. **Avoid:** Wet area that floods in heavy rain, or a service door that becomes a back door for intruders.  
   **Do:** Floor 3 in below the house sloping to its trap; a lockable steel service door to the walkway with a kerb.  
   *Shown on: A-102, A-108*
45. **Avoid:** Washing machine with no tap, drain or socket at the right height.  
   **Do:** Provide a tap at 3 ft, a floor drain behind the machine and a splash-proof 16 A socket at 4 ft.  
   *Shown on: P-101, E-103*
46. **Avoid:** No store, so provisions end up on the floor.  
   **Do:** A 4 x 4'-6" store off the dining with full-height racks, a high jali vent and a jali above the door; lofts over the kitchen.  
   *Shown on: A-104*
47. **Avoid:** Inverter batteries in a bedroom or a closed cupboard.  
   **Do:** Lithium inverter in the ventilated under-stair cupboard, away from water and bedrooms.  
   *Shown on: E-103*

## Pooja
48. **Avoid:** Lamp smoke blackens the ceiling and the teak panelling.  
   **Do:** Fit a heat-proof granite or brass back panel behind the diya and a small high vent; use a washable ceiling finish.  
   *Shown on: A-108*
49. **Avoid:** Pooja sharing a wall with a toilet.  
   **Do:** Here the pooja sits in the living's south-east corner, back to back with the kitchen (no toilet on any side), opening west into the living with doors that open inward.  
   *Shown on: A-102*
50. **Avoid:** Polished marble floor that becomes slippery with oil.  
   **Do:** Use honed (matt) marble or teak.  
   *Shown on: A-108*

## Bedrooms
51. **Avoid:** AC indoor unit above the bed head: cold air blows on your head all night.  
   **Do:** Mount the AC on a side or opposite wall, throwing across the bed.  
   *Shown on: E-103*
52. **Avoid:** Only one switchboard at the door.  
   **Do:** Add 2-way switches at the bed for the light and fan, plus bedside sockets.  
   *Shown on: E-101, E-103*
53. **Avoid:** Wardrobe shallower than 2 ft: hangers do not fit.  
   **Do:** Make wardrobes 2 ft deep with lofts above to the false ceiling.  
   *Shown on: A-104, A-105*
54. **Avoid:** 2'-6" bedroom doors: a mattress or cupboard cannot go in later.  
   **Do:** Make bedroom doors 3'-0".  
   *Shown on: A-401*
55. **Avoid:** Curtain rods, TV brackets and fan hooks screwed into plain AAC, which pulls out.  
   **Do:** Fix timber grounds or use AAC-rated anchors, marked before plaster.  
   *Shown on: A-001*
56. **Avoid:** Windows without mesh, so mosquito coils every night.  
   **Do:** Put insect mesh on a separate frame on every opening.  
   *Shown on: A-401*

## Wardrobes and dressing
57. **Avoid:** Clothes stored in a walk-in only one person can use.  
   **Do:** The upper master has a dressing room between the bedroom and its bath; the ground master and small bedroom have built-in wardrobes with lofts.  
   *Shown on: A-108*
58. **Avoid:** Dressing mirror lit from behind.  
   **Do:** Mount the mirror light above or beside the mirror.  
   *Shown on: E-101, E-102*

## Bathrooms
59. **Avoid:** Raised step at the bathroom door, which people trip on at night.  
   **Do:** Drop the bathroom 12 mm with a sloped granite threshold and no step.  
   *Shown on: A-502/5*
60. **Avoid:** Whole floor sloped to one trap: the entire bathroom is always wet.  
   **Do:** Separate wet and dry zones: a linear drain in the shower, the dry zone nearly flat, and a glass partition.  
   *Shown on: A-502/5*
61. **Avoid:** No waterproofing or flood test: damp skirting in the next bedroom within a year.  
   **Do:** Waterproof the floor, 12 in up the walls and 7 ft in the shower, then do a 48 h flood test before tiling.  
   *Shown on: A-502/5, A-001*
62. **Avoid:** Wooden door frame standing in the wet floor.  
   **Do:** Use a WPC / FRP shutter with the frame on a granite or WPC block.  
   *Shown on: A-401, A-502/9*
63. **Avoid:** Exhaust fan blowing into the false ceiling.  
   **Do:** Duct the exhaust fan straight out through the outer wall.  
   *Shown on: E-101*
64. **Avoid:** Geyser hidden above the false ceiling, which can't be serviced.  
   **Do:** Wall-mount it at 6'-6" with the switch outside; leave a ceiling access panel at the concealed cistern.  
   *Shown on: E-103*
65. **Avoid:** Black or glossy tiles and chrome fittings with Guntur's hard water: white scale shows everywhere.  
   **Do:** Use light matt tiles and matt / PVD fittings, plus a softener if hardness is high.  
   *Shown on: A-108, P-101*
66. **Avoid:** Bath over a bedroom or the living: a leak shows on the ceiling below.  
   **Do:** The upper bath sits exactly over the common bath (wet over wet); waterproof its slab, flood-test it and keep its WC stack in the wet wall.  
   *Shown on: A-109, P-101*
67. **Avoid:** No backing for grab bars later.  
   **Do:** Fix blocking in the walls beside the WC and shower now.  
   *Shown on: A-502/5*

## Study table (small bedroom)
68. **Avoid:** Window behind the screen (silhouette on video calls) or in front of it (glare).  
   **Do:** The study table sits on the south wall with the north window to the side.  
   *Shown on: A-104*
69. **Avoid:** Not enough power or data.  
   **Do:** Fit a UPS socket, a normal socket and a Cat6 point at the table, with the router on the inverter.  
   *Shown on: E-103*

## Doors and windows
70. **Avoid:** Solid walls and closed doors between rooms: no breeze crosses the house at night.  
   **Do:** Put a vent above inner doors where privacy allows, 7'-6" to 9'-0": open jali for the pooja and the store.  
   *Shown on: A-401*
71. **Avoid:** Open vents above bedroom and bathroom doors: conversation, TV and smells carry.  
   **Do:** No vents at bedrooms (windows, fans and AC are enough) or bathrooms (the door's bottom louvre feeds the exhaust fan).  
   *Shown on: A-401*
72. **Avoid:** A long corridor that wastes area.  
   **Do:** Here the lobby is 10 x 3'-6" and the passage beside the stair is 2'-6" and only serves the small bedroom and the linen cupboard.  
   *Shown on: A-106*
73. **Avoid:** No drip groove under sills and sunshades: black streaks down the walls.  
   **Do:** Slope every external sill 1:10 with a drip groove, and give every chajja a drip groove under its edge.  
   *Shown on: A-501/3*
74. **Avoid:** Unseasoned teak that warps and jams.  
   **Do:** Use kiln-seasoned wood, or sal / pinkado frames and UPVC on the wet side.  
   *Shown on: A-401*
75. **Avoid:** Mild-steel hinges and screws that rust with humidity and hard water.  
   **Do:** Use SS 304 hardware, with magnetic stoppers on every door.  
   *Shown on: A-401*
76. **Avoid:** Grill with no openable section: no way out in a fire.  
   **Do:** Give each bedroom one escape window with an openable grill.  
   *Shown on: A-401*

## Floors, walls and finishes
77. **Avoid:** Skirting stuck on top of the plaster: a dust ledge that also chips.  
   **Do:** Set the skirting flush with the plaster face, with a groove above it.  
   *Shown on: A-501/2*
78. **Avoid:** Tiles laid tight to the walls: they pop up (tenting) in 45 C summers.  
   **Do:** Leave 5 mm gaps at walls and movement joints every 15-20 ft, hidden by the skirting.  
   *Shown on: A-501/2, A-108*
79. **Avoid:** Cement grout in bathrooms turns black.  
   **Do:** Use epoxy grout in wet areas.  
   *Shown on: A-001*
80. **Avoid:** Exterior paint that chalks and fades in strong sun.  
   **Do:** Use a waterproof plaster additive and elastomeric or UV-stable exterior paint.  
   *Shown on: A-601*

## Roof and rainwater
81. **Avoid:** Clay tiles just resting on battens: a cyclone strips the edges first.  
   **Do:** Clip or screw every eave, verge, hip and ridge tile, plus every third row elsewhere.  
   *Shown on: A-110, A-501/4*
82. **Avoid:** Water creeping back under the eave tiles and into the soffit.  
   **Do:** Use a fascia with drip-edge flashing turned down into the gutter, and raise the first tile row on a tilting fillet.  
   *Shown on: A-501/4*
83. **Avoid:** No gutters: roof water splashes the plinth and soaks the foundation.  
   **Do:** Gutters on both long eaves, downpipes into the sump filter; overflow to the road drain (no recharge pit near the walls on this narrow plot).  
   *Shown on: A-110, P-101*
84. **Avoid:** Where the low front roof meets the two-storey wall, and at the ventilator: joints sealed only with mortar.  
   **Do:** Use a metal apron flashing chased into the wall with a counter-flashing, and a 9 in upstand with flashing at the ventilator.  
   *Shown on: A-110*
85. **Avoid:** No insulation under Mangalore tiles: rooms radiate heat until midnight.  
   **Do:** Add 50 mm insulation on the deck, ventilate the attic at ridge and soffits, and fit insulated false ceilings in bedrooms.  
   *Shown on: A-301, A-501/4*
86. **Avoid:** Birds, bats and rats nesting in the roof.  
   **Do:** Bird stops at the eaves and mesh at ridge vents and soffits.  
   *Shown on: A-501/4*

## Electrical
87. **Avoid:** One circuit for everything; cheap wire.  
   **Do:** Separate MCBs for each AC, geyser, kitchen and pump; RCCB 30 mA; FR-LSH copper wire sized per load.  
   *Shown on: E-103*
88. **Avoid:** No inverter wiring planned, so it gets rewired later.  
   **Do:** Put lights, fans, office sockets and the router on a separate inverter circuit from day one.  
   *Shown on: E-101, E-103*
89. **Avoid:** AC drain pipe dripping down the wall and sleeves forgotten.  
   **Do:** Fit a 65 mm sleeve sloping out, a drain to a gully, and ODU brackets on shaded walls.  
   *Shown on: E-103*
90. **Avoid:** No spare conduits for solar PV, CCTV, the sump pump and gate.  
   **Do:** Run empty 25 mm conduits to the DB now; it costs little at this stage.  
   *Shown on: E-103, A-110*

## Plumbing and water
91. **Avoid:** Pipes buried under the floor slab: leaks can't be found without breaking tiles.  
   **Do:** Run supply lines along the outside walls in clamps or shallow chases, with a valve per room.  
   *Shown on: P-101*
92. **Avoid:** Water tank too low over a first-floor shower: no pressure.  
   **Do:** A 1,000 L loft tank in the roof space over the upper bath, filled from the sump, plus a small pressure pump for the showers.  
   *Shown on: P-101, A-110*
93. **Avoid:** All waste going into the septic tank, which overloads it.  
   **Do:** Keep soil and grey water separate: soil to the septic, grey to the soak pit or a road drain, with inspection chambers at every bend.  
   *Shown on: P-101*
94. **Avoid:** Drinking borewell water without testing it (fluoride is common around Guntur).  
   **Do:** Test the water; RO at the kitchen for drinking, and a softener if hardness exceeds 300 mg/l.  
   *Shown on: P-101*
95. **Avoid:** Sump lid at ground level: surface water and insects get in.  
   **Do:** Raise the sump cover 6 in above the paving with a locked, sealed lid, and keep it 20 ft+ from the septic.  
   *Shown on: A-101*

## Outside: yard, gate and garden
96. **Avoid:** Gate swinging out onto the road, or too narrow for two-wheelers.  
   **Do:** Use a 7 ft gate swinging inward or sliding, with the meter and bell in the pillar.  
   *Shown on: A-101*
97. **Avoid:** Two-wheeler bay with no slope: puddles.  
   **Do:** Pave it sloping 1:50 toward the road drain.  
   *Shown on: A-101*
98. **Avoid:** No outdoor drying space.  
   **Do:** A fold-away clothesline in the south walkway behind the master, and more room in the spare rear land; the first-floor lounge also dries clothes in the monsoon.  
   *Shown on: A-104*

## Hold points: stop and inspect before work is covered up
| Stage | Check | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Before design freeze | Soil test report, level survey of plot and road, water quality test (TDS, hardness, fluoride) | Owner, engineer |
| Excavation | Footing depth and bearing layer seen in the pit; no black soil left under footings | Structural engineer |
| Before each concrete pour | Reinforcement, cover blocks, laps and column positions checked; photos taken | Engineer / site supervisor |
| Plinth | Anti-termite treatment (certificate), fill compaction, DPC laid and cured | Supervisor |
| Before plaster | Conduits, pipes, AC sleeves, mesh over AAC joints; photos with a tape measure in view | Owner, supervisor |
| Wet rooms | Waterproofing coats, upturns, 48 h flood test passed before any tile is laid | Owner, plumber |
| Roof | Truss holding-down bolts, membrane and insulation, edge tiles clipped, flashings at the ventilator and valleys | Engineer, owner |
| Before handover | Water test on every floor slope and sill, plumbing pressure test, electrical insulation and earth test | Owner, MEP |
