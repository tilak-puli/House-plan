# Noah Daniel review: ground-floor rooms, one by one

Aspect: each ground-floor room on its own, in detail. Sources: `99_Source/model.py` (every coordinate below is from it), `Duplex_Design_Spec.md`, drawing pages A-101 (p.4) and A-104 (p.6). Clear sizes are measured face to face, using the model's wall types: EXT 9 in, WET 7 in, INT 5 in.

---

## 1. Noah's take

"Okay friends, it's floor plan time, and honestly there's a lot to love here. The front door axis, gate to tulsi to steps to door to a lit lamp niche, is *chef's kiss*. The bedrooms come off a side lobby you never see from the front door. And a closed kitchen with a glazed hatch is exactly the 'glass partition' answer I give people who fry and temper. But when I walk it room by room, a few rooms are doing someone else's job.

The master is three bed-lengths long, and the bed is in the wrong half: you walk in at its head, half the headboard floats in the air, and the TV is jammed against a west window.

The dining is a four-seat table in a 5 ft strip, in a house that hosts festivals. Meanwhile a 4 × 4'-6" nook holds one crockery cabinet, while right next door the wet area tries to wash vessels and clothes and be the back door in 25 sq ft.

On the verandah, the swing hits a pillar.

None of this is a teardown. It's 'take a few feet from here, give them to there', and every move fits inside the 22 ft width. Does that make sense?"
