**Noah's overall verdict:** "The bones are good." He keeps all of these:
- public rooms at the front and private ones at the back, with the stair on the hinge between them;
- the master on the ground floor;
- the two stacked wet zones;
- the front covered terrace;
- the stepped roof;
- the gate-to-lamp-niche entry axis;
- the hidden WCs;
- the Bedroom 2 suite entered through its alcove.

What he pushes on: the entry and pooja, the master, the dining, the bathrooms, the stair direction, the terrace frame and the grid.

## B. Big moves: owner decisions

### B1. Pooja to the north-east corner, with a genkan foyer at the door
Three reviewers proposed this independently: flow, layout and ground rooms.

**Today:** anyone praying stands on the kitchen-door threshold, at the start of the busiest path in the house. The shoe bench is in the far NE corner.

**Change:**
- The pooja moves to 57–61 × 21–25, still opening west.
- The old pooja box (57–61 × 12.5–16.5) becomes the shoe alcove and drop zone by the kitchen door.
- A small foyer sits between them, inside the main door.

**Effect:** vastu improves (NE is the ideal pooja corner) and the busy path is cleared. The living seating shifts about 1 ft west.

### B2. The master bedroom: two options
Every reviewer flagged it: a 22 ft room doing three jobs, with the bed actually in the NW and the door landing at the bed head.

- **M1, the zone swap** (ground rooms; cheap, with no structural walls):
  - the bed goes to the true SW, head south, at x 18–24, stacked exactly under Bedroom 2's bed and on the x 21 axis the alignment reviewer wants;
  - an 8 ft wardrobe sits at y 13–15 under the upstairs y 14 beam;
  - the TV, sofa and day bed move to the NW zone by the bath;
  - the small room stays.
- **M2, re-split the west bay at y 14** (layout; bigger):
  - the master in the SW (14–28 × 3–14) with a vestibule (28–34.5 × 9.5–13) and its own new bath (28–36 × 3–9.5) on the south stack;
  - the NW becomes a guest room / den using the existing north bath;
  - **the 10 × 9 small room goes**;
  - about +Rs 1.2–1.8 L for one more bath.

  The vastu consultant needs to confirm the new bath's position.

**My read:** M1 if the small room matters; M2 if a second en-suite and a den matter more.

### B3. Dining light well (the affordable nadumuttam)
Layout and light-and-air both proposed this.

**Today:** the dining is an 11 × 5 ft strip with almost no daylight.

**Change:**
- cut a slab void of about 6 × 4'-6" at x 45.5–51, y 8–12.5, opening to the covered terrace;
- give it a rail, a walk-on jali grating and a closable glazed lid (for the AC and the monsoon);
- a longer extending table then seats 5 daily and 7–8 at festivals.

**Cost:** it needs structural design, and the drying zone moves within the terrace.

### B4. Fold the dining nook into a linear utility
Ground rooms and flow proposed this.

**Change:** the wet area becomes 42–53 × 3–7.5, a straight 11 ft service run, and the dining gets a straight bench wall.

**Cost:** the dining loses its only window, W11, which makes B3 (or a high glazed band) necessary. The fallback is to keep the nook as a tea and water bar.

### B5. Laundry upstairs?
Flow and upper rooms raised this.

**Today:** a wet basket travels about 60 ft and climbs 16 risers to the terrace for drying.

**Options:**
- **A:** the washer goes in the terrace's SW corner against Bath 2's wall, with a supply and trap cast in stage 1.
- **B:** keep it downstairs and dry along the south walkway.

Noah would ask how the family actually does laundry. Your call.

### B6. A door across the bedroom lobby at x 34.5
Flow proposed this, and alignment's "spine" finding supports it.

**Today:** a 33 ft view runs from the living down the hall into the master door.

**Change:** add a door (or cased opening plus door) at x 34.5. Guests keep the stair and the common bath; the master and small room become a closed private wing. Re-hang D02 on its north jamb.

### B7. Low tiled roof wrapping the north, south and west walls (the Kerala chaipu)
Proposed by exterior.

**Change:** a low roof with its eave at +11'-1", level with the gable and wing eaves, gives every side a tiered roof, protects the walls in the monsoon and replaces the window hoods. About Rs 1.5–2.5 L.

**Feasibility check (mine):** the north wall is only 2 ft from the boundary and the main roof eave is already at the line. On the north the chaipu could only be about 1'-6" deep, or none at all. Realistic on the south and west only.

### B8. Back garden court
Proposed by layout.

**Today:** no door leads to the 14 ft of private land behind the house. The septic tank sits in its SW corner, which vastu objects to.

**Change:** wall the back land as a garden court. Move the septic tank and soak pit to the NW. Add a garden door from the master (or the den) onto a stone sit-out under the pergola.

### B9. Close the road side of the terrace
Proposed by exterior.

**Change:** a 2 ft solid upstand plus jali, matching the side parapets. Build all the parapets in stage 1 so the house doesn't look unfinished.

**Trade-off:** less view and breeze from the terrace.

## C. Significant refinements (no rooms move)

**Entry and the grid**
- **C1. One entry axis at y 18.75** (alignment and exterior). The door, steps, path, tulsi and gate are at 19.1, while the gable is at 18.75. Put the pillars at 13.1 / 16.05 / 21.45 / 24.4 so the charupadi seats are equal.
- **C2. Door hierarchy** (alignment and exterior). The main door is 3 ft, the same as the pooja door, and every door head is at 7 ft. Make the main door 4 ft with an 8 ft head and a jali panel above, and the terrace door D36 3'-6" with an 8 ft head (upper rooms).
- **C3. One cross wall line** (alignment). The upstairs wall at y 11.5 sits 6 in off the ground wall at y 12. Put both on y 11.5: the baths stack exactly, the stair hall gains 6 in and the small room loses 6 in.

**Upstairs rooms**
- **C4. Bedroom 2 alcove as a real vestibule** with a linear closet (upper rooms):
  - a hanging run at x 28–30;
  - drawers opposite, with an aisle ending on a re-centred W35;
  - a pocket opening to the bedroom;
  - delete the north wardrobe F90, so the bed foot clear goes from 2'-3" to 3'-11".
- **C5. Study re-furnish** (upper rooms): one linear two-station desk under the north window, a full-height library wall behind as the call backdrop, the sofa-bed in front, and a glazed door.
- **C6. Terrace zones** (upper rooms): laundry and drying in the SW, a square lounge group in the NW, an empty festival floor in the middle, and a planted east edge.

**Light and air**
- **C7. The stair well as the chimney** (light and air): a tall north window column centred on the stair (x 38.25), an upper clerestory at F1 that opens as the outlet, an insulated ceiling over the stair, and the living/stair jali W10 widened to about 4 ft and taken up to the slab.
- **C8. Living daylight** (light and air): a north clerestory band (7'-6" to 9 ft) across x 43–60, a window to the poomukham over the shoe zone, and a fanlight over the main door.
- **C9. Vents above doors** (light and air): closable jali vents over D04, D31 and D35, and louvres over the bath doors, so the rooms breathe into the stair.
- **C10. Roof-space heat** (light and air, medium confidence): the hot layer under the hip (+20'-10" to +28'-4") has no outlet. Add Kerala gablets with jali at both hip ends.

**Kitchen**
- **C11. Kitchen re-detail** (ground rooms): a full-height SE corner unit instead of a blind corner, the hob set clear of the fridge and pantry traffic with proper landing space, the tall group pushed against the east run, and the microwave in the pantry.

**Exterior**
- **C12. Brick for the kitchen front** (exterior): exposed local brick (or solid laterite) wrapped round the corner instead of a laterite veneer on one face, with local granite for the plinth and seat bases.

## D. Moderate items (full detail in the reports)

**Windows and shading**
- Centre the bath windows on the showers: W03 and W33 to x 39.5–41.5, W43 and W42 to x 32–34.
- Make the master's south window W14 a 5 ft low window centred on the bed or day bed, and shrink the west window W01 (west is the hard sun face in Guntur).
- Add hoods to the ground-floor north windows; the eaves only shade upstairs.
- Give the north windows W04 and W09 a jali on their lower 2 ft for privacy, and possibly raise the compound wall to 6'-6".
- Centre the stair window W05 (it's 3 in off).
- Tidy the south façade into two window widths and two sill heights.
- Set the west windows on a y 8.5 / 19.5 grid.

**Doors and circulation**
- Swing the bath doors D07 and D33 inward, with lift-off hinges.
- Move doors jammed 1–2 in from corners.
- Add a scooter gate at y 4–8.
- Screen the poomukham's open north side.
- The service steps fill the 3 ft south walkway.

**Rooms and furniture**
- Living: smaller armchairs (the gaps are 1'-1"), lamp sockets, and one feature on the stair wall instead of three.
- Small room: it has no fan or AC point.
- Master bath shower: only about 2'-9" × 2'-7".
- Under the stair: a dead alcove.
- The lobby cupboard narrows the lobby to 3'-8".

**Exterior details**
- Build the road-face compound wall in brick or stone.
- Make the gable face a teak jali that vents the roof, rather than plaster with three vents.

**Kitchen**
- Kitchen ventilators above the windows and over the hob wall.

## Where the reviewers disagreed

1. **Master bedroom:** M1 (zone swap) versus M2 (re-split) is a real choice; see B2. Alignment's grid fits either, as long as the bed sits at x 18–24.
2. **Dining window versus a merged utility:** B4 removes the dining's only window, so take it only together with B3.
3. **Terrace road side:** exterior wants it closed (B9). The earlier choice was open for view and breeze. Your call.
4. **Bedroom 2 wardrobe F90:** alignment wanted a matching divider stacked under it; upper rooms deletes it (C4). If C4 is taken, the ground divider still aligns with the y 14 beam.

## Rejected by the reviewers (with reasons)

- **Terrace at the back** (layout, exterior): west sun, both bath stacks broken, worse vastu, and phasing over the occupied living.
- **An open-to-sky courtyard** (layout, exterior): no room in a 22 ft wide house. The light well (B3) is the version that fits.
- **A second gable on the main roof** (exterior): it would sit 4'-9" off the entry axis.
- **One roof across the whole front** (exterior): it blocks the terrace view.
- **Swapping Bedroom 2 and the study, or opening the study to the terrace** (upper rooms): stack, vastu and north light.
- **Shrinking the terrace for bigger rooms** (upper rooms): the rooms are already the right size.

## Suggested package (my recommendation)

1. **All of A** (defects).
2. **B1** pooja NE with genkan, and **B2** the master re-plan (M1 or M2: your pick).
3. **B3 + B4** together, only if you accept a slab void.
4. **C1–C6.** Low cost, high payoff.
5. **C7–C9.** Cheap passive cooling, which matters in Guntur.

Leave B5–B9, C10–C12 and section D for a second round, or add the ones you like.
